Stops & Packages

Prototype - two steps, processed locally on your phone.

1. Upload and check your package list

Select multiple screenshots together. Customer/# rows and street-address stops are supported. Overlapping stops and package IDs are merged; conflicts are reported, not duplicated. Screenshot checkmarks do not count as packages found here.

Upload screenshots or type your list below.

    One stop/customer heading followed by package IDs, one per line. Leave a blank line between stops. Each unique package ID counts as one expected bag for that stop. Import all screenshots for a stop before scanning. Review OCR, especially 0/O and customer reference numbers. A # reference is not treated as a stop number.

    2. Scan and group packages

    Scan one Data Matrix or QR code at a time. No camera text recognition is used. The popup shows the stop number and "Bag x of y": unique packages scanned after OK out of the total in your current list, not the printed bag position. Tap OK to count and resume. Rescan never counts a package.

    Camera is off.

    Images and camera frames stay in your browser. Internet is required to download the scanning engines. The list and progress are not saved. Copy your list before closing or refreshing. Leaving this page stops the camera.
